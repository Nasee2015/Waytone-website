Program Details

Designed Specifically for School Campuses

Addressing the greatest modern educational obstacle: excessive screen time, shortened attention spans, and disengaged home environments.

📱

From Passive Screens to Active Skills

Students today spend an average of 4 to 6 hours daily on mobile games and short-form video algorithms. Our program helps students understand how algorithms hold their attention and empowers them to exchange aimless screen time for communication, creative thinking, and sports.

🗣️

Fear-Free Classroom Expression

Many students hesitate to speak in English or participate in class discussions due to fear of mistakes. Through laughter-filled games and expressive challenges, we break this hesitation and build natural speaking confidence that reflects in academic performance.

🤝

Bridging School, Parent & Child

Teachers cannot solve digital distraction alone, and parents are often overwhelmed by daily gadget arguments. Our joint parent session aligns families and schools, establishing healthy gadget-free home zones without conflict or punishment.

Step-by-Step Methodology

Program Structure & Modules

A proven 4-stage progression that takes students from awareness to action, and bridges parents into supportive mentors.

Module 01

The Digital Reality Check (Screen-Time De-Addiction)

Students audit their daily mobile habits without feeling criticized. We teach how social media and gaming algorithms work, and guide students to take ownership of their attention.

  • Honest personal screen-time self-audit
  • Understanding dopamine triggers and infinite scroll traps
  • Practical 'App Swaps' for creative and athletic pursuits
  • Creating phone-free study zones at home
Module 02

Practical Skill Awakening (Communication & Thinking)

Filling reclaimed screen hours with skills that build long-term confidence. Focus on fearless English communication, active listening, and mental agility.

  • Breaking stage hesitation through group communication games
  • Everyday English speaking without worrying about mistakes
  • Mathematical problem solving & logic puzzles
  • General awareness (GK) exploration beyond textbooks
Module 03

The Family Bridge (For Parents & Students)

Empowering parents with gentle, effective communication methods so home becomes an environment of encouragement rather than perpetual phone battles.

  • Eliminating the "daily gadget fight" through collaborative family rules
  • Creating device-free dinner rituals and daily quality time
  • Understanding adolescent stress, exam pressure, and screen triggers
  • Joint parent-child goal setting and shared hobbies
Module 04

The 21-Day Action Blueprint & Certification

Real change happens in the 3 weeks following the orientation. Every student receives a structured physical habit tracker and takes an inspiring personal pledge.

  • Official Waytone Habit Tracking Certificate of Completion
  • Daily physical habit tracker with peer-buddy accountability
  • Classroom teacher evaluation toolkit
  • 21-day milestone check-in report for school coordinators
